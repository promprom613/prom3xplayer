## prom3xplayer by YH

A light, fast, ad-free music player. Download the APK below and install it
(uninstall older builds first, the signing key changes between builds).

### Highlights
- White heart in the player: like a song and its title turns bright pink everywhere
- Swipe a song left to like it, right to play it next (swiped songs line up in order)
- Plus button in the player to add the song to a playlist
- "Liked" option in the sort menu
- Pause when the volume hits 0, resume when it goes back up (switchable in Settings)
- Custom playlist covers
- Set any song as ringtone or notification sound
- Full in-app tutorial for every gesture and hidden feature
- Always-black status bar, new icon, custom About page
- Smoother first visit to Settings

Based on Gramophone (GPL-3.0). Thank you to its authors and contributors.
